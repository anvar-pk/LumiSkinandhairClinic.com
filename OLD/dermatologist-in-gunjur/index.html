Skin and hair care in Gunjur

If you are looking for a dermatologist near Gunjur or Varthur, Lumi Skin & Hair Clinic offers consultations for common skin, scalp and hair concerns, as well as selected cosmetic dermatology procedures. Treatment recommendations are personalised after a clinical assessment.

Medical dermatology

Assessment and care for acne, eczema, psoriasis, fungal infections, pigmentation concerns, urticaria and other skin conditions.

Cosmetic dermatology

Consultations for uneven skin tone, melasma, tanning, freckles and other cosmetic concerns. Suitability is assessed before treatment.

Laser hair reduction

Hair and scalp care
